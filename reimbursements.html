Reimbursement Requests

Thank you for making a purchase on behalf of KCUDA! Submit your request through our online form and choose how you would like to be paid back.

Open the reimbursement form →

Before you start

  • Have your itemized receipts ready as photos or PDFs.
  • Know what the purchase was for (the event, program or committee).
  • Decide how you would like to be paid: Zelle, Venmo, PayPal or check.

How to submit

  1. Open the link.
  2. Enter your name and email.
  3. For each expense, add the date, vendor, amount and a short description.
  4. Upload a receipt for every item.
  5. Designate how you would like to be paid:
    6. Zelle:
    The email or phone number linked to your Zelle account
    Venmo:
    Your Venmo username (@handle)
    PayPal:
    The email linked to your PayPal account
    Check:
    Mailing address (for mailed paper check)
  6. Check the total and click Submit. You'll get an email saying your request was received.

After you submit

Your request will be reviewed and approved, or followed up with questions if applicable.

60 days

Please submit within 60 days of purchase

~10 days

Payment sent within ~10 days of approval
