Winter League

A community youth ultimate frisbee league in Brooklyn for middle and high school players — think AYSO, not a school team. Not affiliated with any school. Registration and league details are coming soon.

New to ultimate? Learn to Play

About the league

KCUDA's Winter League is an in-house, community-based league for middle and high school players. It is open to students from any school — our players have come from Brooklyn Tech and many other schools across the city.

The league is run by KCUDA, not by a school, and it runs through March 15.

Where Our Players Come From

Our players have come from Brooklyn Technical High School, Stuyvesant High School, LaGuardia High School, Edward R. Murrow High School, Brooklyn Prospect, Midwood High School, Frank Sinatra School of the Arts, Professional Performing Arts School, ICE, BUGS, Park Slope Collegiate, Trinity, MS 51, MS 839, and Mark Twain MS — and we welcome students from every school.
