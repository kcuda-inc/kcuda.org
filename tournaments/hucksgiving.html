About the Tournament

The Hucksgiving Showdown is a single-day high school team-based ultimate tournament at the Parade Grounds in Brooklyn, NY. This tournament brings together youth ultimate teams from the region for a full day of play across four fields.

The event hosts up to 16 teams total. Brooklyn Disco Tech and Brooklyn Tech Support play free; outside teams pay a flat entry fee. Registration will open soon.

Tournament Details

Tournament Format

  • • Full day of play: 8am to 5pm
  • • Fields: Prospect Park Parade Grounds, Fields 7, 8, 10 and 11 (4 fields)
  • • Capacity: Up to 16 teams

Eligibility

  • • Any player registered for BDT-RPP, BDT-LTP, BTS-RPP, or BTS-LTP is eligible — no additional gating
  • • USA Ultimate membership required for the event roster: RPP players are already USAU members; LTP players must join USA Ultimate (at their own cost) before being added to the roster
